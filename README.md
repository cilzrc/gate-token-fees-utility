A tier can be triggered by any one of three separate qualifying routes:

- **30-day total trading volume**, weighted across products (spot counts fully, futures at 40%, options and USD1 futures at 20%, CFDs at 10%),
- **14-day average GT holdings**, where the balance is measured daily and includes GT2, so staked GT still counts,
- **Upgrade asset value**, based on the dollar value of assets you hold, with per-coin weighting.

## What GT does once it's sitting in your account

Four uses that are live right now, ranked roughly by how likely they are to apply to you.

**Fee deduction.** Turn on GT deduction and the platform pays spot fees from your GT balance first. At VIP 0 that's a cut from 0.1% to 0.09%. Higher up, the gap widens: at VIP 9 it's 0.07%/0.075% standard versus 0.05%/0.068% in GT, and from VIP 10 the maker fee can reach zero on the standard schedule. The catch is that GT has to be sitting there. If the balance runs out, the system quietly falls back to the standard rate.

**Staking for GT2.** GT staking converts your GT into GT2 at a 1:1 ratio, pays rewards daily in GT2, and lets you redeem back to GT at 1:1 whenever you want. The minimum is 1 GT. Quoted APR has bounced around depending on which regional page you read, from about 0.79% to 0.97%, with the US page showing 0.86% at the time of writing, and roughly 40 million GT staked in total. The rate is modest, and the more useful angle is that staked GT2 still counts toward the 14-day average holdings snapshot for VIP purposes.

**Launchpool and HODLer allocations.** Holding or staking assets lets you farm rewards. Recent GT rounds have taken BTC or ETH stakes and paid out GT, with advertised APRs in the low-to-mid double digits depending on the round and pool. Minimums have been tiny, in the range of 0.000001 BTC. Rates and pool sizes change every round, so treat any quoted APR as valid for that round only.

**Gas on Gate Layer.** Gate has been positioning GT as the gas token for its own Gate Layer network, which is the main argument for GT demand beyond trading fees. It's an ecosystem play, not a guaranteed revenue stream, and it should be weighed as such.
